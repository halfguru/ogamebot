<context>
The ROI calculator is the mathematical heart of the auto-build system. Per RESEARCH.md, all formulas are verified from ogamed source code (alaingilbert/ogame). The calculator must be pure Go with no I/O — only math functions taking levels, research, temperature, and universe speed as inputs.

Key formulas verified from ogamed source:
- Cost: baseCost × factor^(level-1)
- Metal production: 30 × (1 + plasmaTech/100) × speed × level × 1.1^level + 30×speed
- Crystal production: 20 × speed × (1 + plasmaTech×0.0066) × level × 1.1^level + 15×speed
- Deuterium: 10 × (1 + plasmaTech×0.0033) × level × 1.1^level × (-0.004×avgTemp + 1.36) × speed
- Mine energy: ceil(10 × level × 1.1^level), deuterium mine uses 20 base
- Solar: floor(20 × level × 1.1^level)
- Fusion: round(30 × level × (1.05 + energyTech×0.01)^level)
- Build time: (metalCost + crystalCost) / (2500 × (1 + roboticsFactory) × speed × 2^naniteFactory) hours

Building definitions (from RESEARCH.md):
- MetalMine: ID=1, baseMetal=60, baseCrystal=15, factor=1.5, energyConsumer=true
- CrystalMine: ID=2, baseMetal=48, baseCrystal=24, factor=1.6, energyConsumer=true
- DeuteriumSynthesizer: ID=3, baseMetal=225, baseCrystal=75, factor=1.5, energyConsumer=true
- SolarPlant: ID=4, baseMetal=75, baseCrystal=30, factor=1.5, energyConsumer=false (produces energy)
- FusionReactor: ID=12, baseMetal=900, baseCrystal=360, baseDeuterium=180, factor=1.8, energyConsumer=false

The builder package follows the "pure calculator + impure worker" split pattern. roi.go is the pure part.
</context>
